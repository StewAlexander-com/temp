Location help & data

In Facebook on iPhone, use the app menu to open this page in Safari if device location is blocked. Desktop viewers may also block location. You can always choose a city here when search is available.

Home-screen apps may need their own location permission. A chosen city is remembered in this browser when storage is available; choose it again if another browser or home-screen app starts empty.

City searches go to Open-Meteo (place data by GeoNames). Weather coordinates go to NWS, with Open-Meteo as a fallback. Estimates are labeled. Recent readings and your chosen place are stored on this device. Fresh weather requires internet access.

Your location is used only to request local weather from the National Weather Service.
